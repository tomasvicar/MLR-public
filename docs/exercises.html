Exercises

Computer labs: assignments, notebooks, data

Every lab has an assignment page (what you will learn, the tasks, the small example to compute by hand, data) and, for labs 1–4, 6–9 and 11, a notebook in Google Colab (opens from the assignment page; save a copy to your Drive before editing). Labs 5 and 10 happen in a terminal and an editor and come with a starter project instead; lab 12 is the project consultation.

In the first four labs, switch the AI assistance in Colab off (Settings → AI assistance) — you write the code to understand the algorithm. From lab 5 on, a coding agent is part of the work.

Labs 2026/27

Exercise 1

Introduction, libraries, nearest neighbour

NumPy, Matplotlib, pandas and scikit-learn in an hour. Measure leaves, compute the nearest-neighbour decision by hand, then in NumPy.

Assignment Colab

Exercise 2

k-NN as an object, metrics, splitting, standardization

From NN to k-NN, the scikit-learn estimator interface, metrics from a confusion matrix by hand and in NumPy, hold-out split, standardization, and a broken pipeline to diagnose.

not published yet

Exercise 3

Feature selection

Tables in pandas. Correlation, entropy and mutual information by hand on eight rows, then in NumPy and checked against scipy. PCA from the covariance matrix. Forward selection from scratch and in scikit-learn, backward elimination.

not published yet

Exercise 4

Linear, ridge and kernel regression

Least squares on three points by hand. Polynomial features and why degree 15 goes wrong. Ridge with λ traced in a table before the plot; primal vs. dual; the kernel trick, kernel ridge and kernel PCA; lasso vs. ridge.

not published yet

Exercise 5 · new

Coding agents and Git

How software is written now. Installing a coding agent, Git as the safety net from here on: commits, diffs, going back when the agent breaks something. A starter project you extend with an agent — and a trap in it that only reading the diff will catch.

not published yet

Exercise 6

SVM, decision trees, random forest

SVM primal and dual, the effect of C and of the kernel. One CART split by hand with Gini and entropy, then trees, bagging and random forest in scikit-learn. Pixel-wise segmentation of aerial images. Grid vs. Bayesian search, and a first task handed to an agent.

not published yet

Exercise 7

Neural network from scratch

One learning step of a single neuron by hand, the lecture’s own numbers. A neuron and then a two-layer network in NumPy with backpropagation written out in matrix form and checked with finite differences. Activations and the learning rate.

not published yet

Exercise 8

PyTorch

Tensors and what autograd actually does; last week’s NumPy network rebuilt in PyTorch and checked against your own backpropagation. Optimizers and their state, batches, nn.Module. One 3 × 3 convolution by hand, then Dataset, DataLoader and a CNN on photos of leaves.

not published yet

Exercise 9

Deep-learning showcase with an agent

Seven tasks, one encoder: image and signal classification, segmentation, image-to-image and signal-to-signal regression. Softmax and cross-entropy by hand, one reference task (ECG noise type) worked through, then you pick a task, hand the specification to an agent and validate what comes back.

not published yet

Exercise 10 · new

Practical application on MetaCentrum

Why a notebook stops being enough once a run takes hours. A training project as scripts with arguments, checkpoints and logs, run first locally and then as a GPU job on MetaCentrum: account, job script, scratch, results. Extended with an agent and committed to Git.

not published yet

Exercise 11

Transformers

Scaled dot-product attention for three tokens by hand. Tokenization, positional encoding and a small language model trained on WikiText-2, then last year’s checkpoint generating text. An image as a sequence of patches, a pre-trained vision transformer and its fine-tuning; a transformer on ECG as an optional extra.

not published yet

Exercise 12 · new

Project

Consultation and presentation of your own project: what is fixed (one metric, a hidden test set, a repository with history, a five-minute talk), what is still to be decided, and the checklist before hand-in.

not published yet
