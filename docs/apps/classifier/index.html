How to export your model 
import json, onnx, torch
model.eval().cpu()
torch.onnx.export(model, (torch.zeros(1, 3, 96, 96),), "my_model.onnx",
                  input_names=["image"], output_names=["logits"],
                  external_data=False)
info = {"classes": train_dataset.classes, "input_size": 96,
        "mean": [0.485, 0.456, 0.406], "std": [0.229, 0.224, 0.225]}
m = onnx.load("my_model.onnx")
m.metadata_props.add(key="model_info", value=json.dumps(info))
onnx.save(m, "my_model.onnx")

Then copy my_model.onnx to your phone (e.g. through Google Drive or iCloud Drive) and choose it above.
